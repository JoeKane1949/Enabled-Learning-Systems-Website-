ELS Roadmap

The ELS Roadmap: Blueprint for Continuous Improvement

The ELS Roadmap is a progressive learning framework designed to build a sustainable continuous improvement culture. It guides teams and leaders step-by-step through foundational concepts, technical methodologies like process mapping and problem-solving, and advanced leadership execution.

Stage 1 · Foundation

Foundational Concepts

Basic concepts and values necessary to sustain a Continuous Improvement culture. Includes values, muda/muri/mura, hansei-kay (review meetings), etc.

Stage 2 · Core Practices

TPS Basics

The thinking-way of Toyota Production System and Continuous Improvement. Includes Toyota Production System simulation.

Requires: Foundational Concepts

A3 Communication

The basic concept of using an A3 for good communication and presentations to leadership and others.

Requires: Foundational Concepts

Process Mapping

Visualize processes for standardization and to identify problems.

Requires: Foundational Concepts

Team Basics

Create a workplace where servant leadership and continuous improvement thrives.

Requires: Foundational Concepts

CI for Leaders

Continuous improvement through customer-first empowerment.

Requires: Foundational Concepts
Stage 3 · Applied Skills

Standardization

Tools for creating consistent standards across processes.

Requires: Foundational Concepts, Process Mapping

8 Step Problem Solving

Solving problems at the root cause level.

Requires: Foundational Concepts, A3 Communication
Stage 4 · Advanced Practice

On the Job Development

Coaching team members through CI activities.

Requires: Foundational Concepts, A3 Communication, Process Mapping, Standardization, 8 Step Problem Solving

Strategic Planning

Clarifying and aligning operational goals for organizational cascade and alignment.

Requires: Foundational Concepts, 8 Step Problem Solving
